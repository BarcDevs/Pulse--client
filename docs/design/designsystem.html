07 · Assistant

Voice & Tone

A compassionate recovery companion — warm, supportive, never clinical. Short replies that end with a gentle next step.

Do

  • Name the feeling back: "Restlessness is very common."
  • Offer one concrete option or a choice of two
  • Celebrate small steps
  • Point to Crisis / 988 when risk appears

Don't

  • Diagnose or give clinical advice
  • Lecture or stack multiple questions
  • Use emoji or exclamation-heavy cheer
  • Refer to itself as a person or therapist

09 · Patterns

Layout & Behavior

Conventions that thread the prototype together.

App shell

256px fixed sidebar on the left for every authenticated screen. Content area at marginLeft: 256 with its own scroll container. Auth screens (landing, login, signup, forgot) are full-bleed.

Active nav state

Background sidebarAccent, foreground sidebarAccentFg, weight 600, radius 8. Hover uses the same tint at 50% opacity.

Page header

Optional eyebrow pill → h1 (36px / 800 / -0.02em) → subtitle (15px mutedFg). Lives inside a white card with a bottom border.

Cards over shadows

Default surface is a 1px border in #e2e8f0 with optional resting shadow 0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.04). Heavier drops only for modals.

Focus & hover

Inputs lift their border to primary with a 3px ring at 15% opacity. Cards never scale — they only tint or darken borders.

Routing

Single-page React. Page state is a string held in root App, persisted to localStorage under pulse_page. Children receive an onNavigate(id) prop.
